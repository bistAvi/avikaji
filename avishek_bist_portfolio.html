About Me

Engineer. Researcher.
Problem Solver.

I'm a BS Electrical Engineering graduate from NYU Abu Dhabi (Class of 2025) with a GPA of 3.851, recognized as a University Honors Scholar. My work sits at the intersection of energy systems, photonics research, and data-driven analysis.

From interning at a 111 MW hydropower plant in the Himalayas to simulating neuromorphic photonic circuits in Abu Dhabi, I thrive in environments that demand both deep technical rigor and creative thinking.

Currently, I'm applying machine learning and geospatial analytics to flood risk modeling and water resource management in Nepal, helping make data-driven infrastructure decisions that impact real communities.

When I'm not debugging code or writing simulation scripts, I care deeply about renewable energy and building technology that has a positive impact on the world — especially in underserved regions.
